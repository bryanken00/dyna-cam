Venues can run Open Play: drop-in sessions on some of their courts, once or every week, with a
level range, a cap on players and an optional fee. Players find them in the player app under
Discover → Open Play, see who's going, and join (free sessions at once, paid ones online). They
can cancel before the venue's cut-off for a refund. Staff see who's coming, add walk-ins, check
players in, and cancel a spot or a whole session; paid players are refunded automatically. A
session's courts can't be booked while it runs.

## Dev notes

- back/: run `npm run db:migrate` (050: `play_series`, `play_series_courts`, `play_sessions`,
  `play_session_courts`, `play_signups`, `court_closures.playSessionId`, `payments.playSignupId`,
  the `open_play` permission granted to every Owner). Give staff roles Open Play access in
  Roles; demo roles have it after `npm run db:data`.
- back/: new `/api/v1/admin/open-play/*`, `/api/v1/public/open-play[/:playToken]` and
  `/api/v1/player/play/*`. Paid spots use Xendit invoices `play-<signupCode>` on the venue's
  company account (the same webhooks; nothing to configure). The minute job now also expires
  unpaid spots, sends Open Play reminders, and makes each weekly series' sessions 4 weeks ahead
  (once an hour). Tests: `tests/open-play.test.js`.
- A session blocks its courts as court closures tied to it. They don't appear in, and can't be
  changed from, the Closures list.
- front/: Admin → DGSports → Open Play (`open_play` permission). Payments lists Open Play
  payments; they're refunded by cancelling the spot.
- web-app/: Discover → Open Play (`/open-play`), the session page (`/open-play/:playToken`), and
  my spots on the Play tab.
