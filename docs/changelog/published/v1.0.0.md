# Version 1.0.0

**Released:** September 10, 2026

Our first release. You can set up the companies that use the platform, and each company can manage
its own team and decide who has access to what, all in one place.

## ✨ What's new

**For platform administrators (Super Admin)**

- Add and manage the companies that use the platform, along with their branches.
- See platform activity at a glance on the dashboard, including recently added companies.
- Manage user accounts across the platform.

**For company administrators (Admin)**

- Add team members, update their details, and deactivate their accounts when they leave.
- Create roles such as "Cashier" or "Branch Manager" and choose exactly which pages each role can
  view or edit.
- Check the activity log to see who changed what, and when.
- Update your own profile and password from Account Settings.

**For everyone**

- Secure sign-in, with a "Forgot password" option if you can't get into your account.
- Light and dark mode. Switch whenever you like.
- Phone numbers are entered and shown in one consistent format, for example 0912 3456 789.
