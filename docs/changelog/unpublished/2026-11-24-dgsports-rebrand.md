Dyna Cam is now **DGSports**: a new name, the DGPlay logo, its blue-and-lime colours and icons
across the website, the player pages, the staff Admin, printed signs and emails, and a short
opening animation of the logo the first time the site opens in a browser tab. Downloaded game videos are now named "dgsports-game…".

## Dev notes

- front/: `VITE_APP_NAME=DGSports` (set it in `front/.env`); new `components/brand/DGMark`
  (the DGPlay logo), `Wordmark`, `appName.js` (`CourtMark` removed); `index.css` tokens retuned to
  the DGPlay colours (light: cool-white page, blue buttons; dark: `#0B2333` page, lime buttons);
  `--font-display` Space Grotesk. New dependency `@fontsource-variable/space-grotesk` — run
  `npm install`.
- front/: the logo and every icon (`public/brand/*.svg`, the transparent favicon SVG — blue frame,
  white in a dark scheme — + 32 px, Apple touch, mask icon, 192/512 + maskable on the blue tile)
  are generated by `node scripts/brand-icons.mjs` from
  `scripts/brand/dgplay.mjs`; `site.webmanifest`.
- front/: splash = `#dg-splash` in `index.html` (CSS bounce-in, skipped before paint once seen in
  the tab) + `components/splash/startSplash.js` (started from `main.jsx`).
- back/: set `APP_NAME=DGSports`, `MAIL_FROM=DGSports <…>`, `ALERT_DISCORD_USERNAME=DGSports` in
  `back/.env` (defaults changed too). Google Drive folders for **new** courts are named
  "DGSports · venue · court" (existing folders keep their names); video downloads are
  `dgsports-game[-camN].mp4` / `dgsports-replay.mp4`.
- Not in this change: the mobile app and recorder box (still Dyna Cam); the kit's offline page
  (needs a service worker).
