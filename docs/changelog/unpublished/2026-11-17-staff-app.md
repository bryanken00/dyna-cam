The Dyna Cam staff app now covers the court-side jobs: see every court's camera and recorder
status, save a court's last 60 seconds and show the player a QR of their clip, browse and share
clips, book, cancel or end session recordings, take cash payments by pay code, and check the
recorder boxes. The home screen shows today's clips, unpaid clips, sessions recording now and
any recorder box that is offline. Each person sees only what their role allows.

## Dev notes

- `app/`: new dependency `toqr` (pure JavaScript, no native code — no new dev build needed).
- `app/`: permissions are loaded from `/api/v1/admin/permissions/user` after sign-in and are no
  longer kept in the saved login; signing out clears all cached data.
- `app/`: the template's example module and example SQLite table were removed.
- Not yet tried on a phone; "Scan court QR" is not built (needs `expo-camera` + a new dev build).
